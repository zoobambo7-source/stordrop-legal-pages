Refund Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  StoreDrop

Short version: We offer a 7-day refund window for genuine issues. Because StoreDrop is a digital product that is delivered instantly, refunds are handled case by case — not automatically. If something isn't working for you, reach out first.

Our Approach

We stand behind StoreDrop and want you to be happy with your purchase. Digital products work differently from physical goods — once the files are delivered, they can't be "returned." That said, we believe in being fair and we'll always try to make things right.

Refund Window

Refund requests are accepted within 7 days of your purchase date. Requests made after 7 days will not be eligible for a refund.

When We Will Refund

✓ Eligible

StoreDrop does not work as described and we are unable to resolve the issue after reasonable support attempts.

✓ Eligible

You were charged twice or there was a duplicate transaction error.

✓ Eligible

You purchased by mistake and have not downloaded or used the files.

✓ Eligible

A critical feature described on the product page does not function and cannot be fixed.

When We Will Not Refund

✗ Not Eligible

You changed your mind after downloading and using the files.

✗ Not Eligible

The issue is caused by your own hosting setup, third-party service (Supabase, Vercel, etc.), or custom modifications you made.

✗ Not Eligible

You did not read the product description or setup instructions before purchasing.

✗ Not Eligible

Your request is made after the 7-day window.

How to Request a Refund

Contact us through one of these channels:

  • Reply to your Gumroad purchase confirmation email
  • Use Gumroad's buyer messaging system on your purchase receipt

Please include your order number, the email used to purchase, and a brief description of the issue. We aim to respond within 48 hours.

Try Support First

Before requesting a refund, please reach out for support. Most issues (Supabase connection, file not loading, mobile display) can be resolved quickly. We genuinely want StoreDrop to work for you.

Processing

Approved refunds are processed through Gumroad and typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your payment method and bank. Gumroad handles the actual refund transaction.

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this refund policy at any time. The policy in effect at the time of your purchase applies to that purchase.
