Your privacy matters. StoreDrop is a self-hosted storefront tool — we do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. This policy explains what data is handled and by whom.

1. Who We Are

StoreDrop is a single-file digital storefront product. When you purchase StoreDrop, you receive software files that you host yourself. The operator of this store and the StoreDrop product is an independent creator, not a corporation.

2. What Data Is Collected

StoreDrop itself does not collect any personal data from your store's visitors. When you deploy StoreDrop with Supabase, the following data may be stored in your own Supabase database:

  • Product listings you create (names, prices, descriptions, images)
  • Store settings you configure (store name, colors, social links)
  • Anonymous analytics events (product views and buy-clicks — no personal identifiers)

All of this data is stored in your own Supabase project, which you control. We have no access to it.

3. Purchase Data (Gumroad)

StoreDrop is sold via Gumroad. When you purchase, Gumroad collects your name, email address, and payment details as part of the checkout process. This is governed by Gumroad's Privacy Policy. We receive your email address from Gumroad solely to deliver your purchase and provide support.

4. How We Use Your Email

If you purchase StoreDrop, your email may be used to:

  • Deliver your purchased files
  • Send product updates or critical security notices
  • Respond to support requests you initiate

We do not add you to marketing lists without explicit consent. We do not sell or share your email with any third party.

5. Cookies & Tracking

The StoreDrop storefront does not use cookies or third-party tracking scripts by default. If you optionally configure an external analytics service (such as Plausible or Fathom) in your storedrop-config.js, that service's own privacy policy applies to your visitors.

6. Third-Party Services

StoreDrop integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Database and file storage (your own account). See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — Font loading via CDN. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN — Stylesheet delivery only, no personal data collected.

7. Data Retention

We retain purchase records only as long as required for business and legal purposes. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Children's Privacy

StoreDrop is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
